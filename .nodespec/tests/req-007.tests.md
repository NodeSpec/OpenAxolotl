# Test Plan: REQ-007 - Consistent recovery, capabilities and saved progress

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Consistent recovery, capabilities and saved progress
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Preserve existing regeneration/capability, lives/checkpoint and Gill Mod concepts behind explicit public interfaces. Unexpected fallthrough is a defect, not an intended difficulty mechanic.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-007-1** [automated] [PENDING] Checkpoint activation and respawn restore position, velocity and the documented capability/life snapshot without leaving attack, capture or dance states active.
- **AC-REQ-007-2** [automated] [PENDING] Restoration, completion, unique discoveries and dance unlock persist through save/reload under documented namespaces.
- **AC-REQ-007-3** [automated] [PENDING] Unavailable abilities or reduced capability do not softlock the first adventure; mandatory recovery paths remain reachable.
- **AC-REQ-007-4** [manual] [PENDING] Document exactly which encounters can remove capability or consume a life; neither the future whirlpool nor future suit powers are treated as implemented requirements.
- **AC-REQ-007-5** [automated] [PENDING] Riverheart progress distinguishes captive, freed/carried and returned/restored states; checkpoint reset, reload and migration from the old seed-gate save cannot lose the artifact, duplicate it or permanently lock the finale.
