# Test Plan: REQ-003 - Readable camera, controls and accessible feedback

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Readable camera, controls and accessible feedback
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Support keyboard/mouse and gamepad on desktop with clear camera framing, input prompts and multiple feedback channels.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-003-1** [automated] [PENDING] Every mandatory action is available on keyboard/mouse and gamepad; remapped inputs update the prompts.
- **AC-REQ-003-2** [automated] [PENDING] The follow camera handles terrain obstruction and underwater passages without clipping through blocking geometry or losing the player.
- **AC-REQ-003-3** [manual] [PENDING] Mandatory routes, enemy warnings and restoration changes remain understandable without relying only on color or audio; music/effects volume and reduced-motion options are reviewed.
- **AC-REQ-003-4** [automated] [PENDING] Input is sampled independently of presentation, normalized to semantic actions and consumed once per physics tick; dead zones, remapping and focus loss cannot create repeated presses or stuck movement.
- **AC-REQ-003-5** [automated] [PENDING] Camera collision and framing use the same reference traversal through the inlet, root tunnel and terrace edges; changing render FPS cannot create a one-frame camera teleport except an explicit respawn cut.
- **AC-REQ-003-6** [manual] [PENDING] Approve controller feel and camera framing in the same character reel, including a reduced-motion profile, before expanding world dressing.
