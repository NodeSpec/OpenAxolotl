# Test Plan: REQ-013 - Human review before community publication

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Human review before community publication
**Category:** functional
**Description:** The project owner controls official acceptance and release, with explicitly delegated and revocable reviewer/publisher roles. Private parent-led submissions enter a review queue by type (asset, character or playable level); automated checks and human review are both required. Reusable-content approval and complete-level approval are separate decisions, and contributors receive actionable feedback.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-013-1** [automated] [PENDING] Official publication requires passing automated checks and recorded owner-authorized human approval for the exact submission type, identity, version, content digest and pinned dependencies. Contributors and automated agents cannot approve or publish their own submissions without explicit owner delegation; an asset/character reuse approval cannot authorize a playable level.
- **AC-REQ-013-2** [manual] [PENDING] Human review checks age appropriateness for children aged 5–9, cohesive art and gameplay, playability, recovery, clarity, accessibility, rights and measured performance on the declared hardware tier. Asset/character reviews include animation, materials, collision and supported behavior; level reviews play the complete route and its assembled content. Names, descriptions, thumbnails and audio are included.
- **AC-REQ-013-3** [automated] [PENDING] Changing submitted content, public-facing metadata or pinned dependencies invalidates the applicable approval and requires a new version/review. Draft, changes-requested and rejected submissions are excluded from public catalogs; review decisions record reviewer identity, scope, reason and time, and only authorized owners/delegates may change release status.
- **AC-REQ-013-4** [manual] [PENDING] Credits, contributor permissions and parent-led submission instructions are understandable before submission.
