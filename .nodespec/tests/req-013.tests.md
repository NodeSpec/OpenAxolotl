# Test Plan: REQ-013 - Flagship Boss Encounter

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Flagship Boss Encounter
**Category:** functional
**Description:** The level's CLIMAX, and the mechanic that ties boss design directly to Pillar 3. The Flagship is the source vessel anchoring a region's Drift Fleet presence — the largest piece of extraction machinery, still faceless, still unmanned. Its finishing moves are one of the few life-costing catastrophic events, while its ordinary attacks strip capability like any other Drift Fleet unit, so the fight escalates along both layers at once.

IT GOES AT THE END OF A LEVEL, and that is a design rule rather than a staging preference. A level is a run of acts, each introducing something new and then asking for it under pressure; the Flagship is what asks for all of it at once. Placed in the middle it is an obstacle and everything after it is anticlimax. Placed last it is the payoff — and because defeating it flips the region from barren to restorable, the restoration reward lands on the last thing the player did rather than on an errand they ran an hour earlier. The encounter requires both movement grammars and the Gill Mod its world emphasizes, because by the end of the level the player has all of them.

WHERE IT DOES NOT GO, STATED SO NOBODY RE-DERIVES IT.

  * NOT IN THE OPEN LAGOON HUB. The hub is the place between levels: a calm, safe space holding portals, where the player arrives, chooses and returns. It is not a level, it declares no world manifest and therefore has no `boss` element to declare, and putting a set-piece fight in it would break the one job it has — being somewhere you are not being attacked. This is structural today and must stay a rule rather than an accident of the hub not being a world module.

  * NOT IN EVERY WORLD. At MVP exactly one world hosts a Flagship: Coral Cove, the long build the whole level ladder leads to. Bubble Bay ships without one (REQ-028), and `boss` stays an OPTIONAL Level Contract element so a short or experimental level remains valid without it — a path the reference template and the conforming-world fixture both prove on every pull request.

Until a shipped world actually reaches this encounter in play it is verified in fixtures only, which is not verified.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-013-1** [automated] [VERIFIED] Flagship ordinary attacks strip capability, and its designated finishing moves decrement a life
- **AC-REQ-013-2** [automated] [VERIFIED] Defeating the Flagship sets its region's restoration unlocked flag, permitting restoration to begin
- **AC-REQ-013-3** [automated] [VERIFIED] The encounter contains at least one mandatory phase completable only in the water grammar and at least one completable only in the land grammar
- **AC-REQ-013-4** [automated] [PENDING] The encounter contains at least one mandatory phase gated on the Gill Mod its world requires
- **AC-REQ-013-5** [automated] [VERIFIED] Checkpoints within the encounter restore lives and capability state and resume the encounter at the last completed phase rather than from its start
- **AC-REQ-013-6** [automated] [VERIFIED] A world declaring no boss remains contract-valid and fully completable
- **AC-REQ-013-7** [automated] [PENDING] The Flagship is the final encounter of the level that hosts it: no mandatory traversal, collectible or region objective remains after it, and the world's finish condition is reached only once the encounter completes
- **AC-REQ-013-8** [automated] [PENDING] Coral Cove declares a boss and reaches the encounter in normal play, proven by the same flown-route probe that proves the rest of that level
- **AC-REQ-013-9** [automated] [PENDING] The Open Lagoon hub hosts no Flagship and no Drift Fleet encounter of any kind, and a test asserts the hub scene declares neither
- **AC-REQ-013-10** [manual] [PENDING] The encounter reads as a climactic set piece and its restoration payoff lands
