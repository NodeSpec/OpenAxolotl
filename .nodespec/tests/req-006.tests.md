# Test Plan: REQ-006 - Glowing crayfish and an optional dance reward

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Glowing crayfish and an optional dance reward
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Blue shore and underwater pickups are recognizable animated crayfish. Their reward adds personality without blocking adventure progress.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-006-1** [automated] [PENDING] Crayfish move within authored shore or underwater habitats with their pickup trigger aligned to the visible creature; they cannot escape into inaccessible terrain.
- **AC-REQ-006-2** [automated] [PENDING] Collecting five unique glowing crayfish unlocks a reusable dance; repeated collection of the same identity cannot increase the saved total.
- **AC-REQ-006-3** [automated] [PENDING] Dance starts only on a flat, clear shore while stationary; movement, water entry, damage, capture or checkpoint reset cancels it. It grants no invulnerability and consumes no collected crayfish.
- **AC-REQ-006-4** [manual] [PENDING] The hero stands on hind legs for the line-dance-inspired routine with a separate groove cue; ordinary background music remains unobtrusive and has independent volume control.

## Criterion Assessment

2 of 4 criteria have potential quality issues:

**AC-REQ-006-1:**
- Warning: Time/size constraint without a numeric value
- Warning: No clear trigger or action verb -- hard to derive a test case
- Suggestion: Add a specific threshold (e.g., 'within 200ms', 'under 5MB')

**AC-REQ-006-2:**
- Warning: No clear trigger or action verb -- hard to derive a test case
- Suggestion: Rewrite in 'When [trigger], [system] shall [observable outcome]' form
