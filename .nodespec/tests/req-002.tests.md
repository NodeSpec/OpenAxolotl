# Test Plan: REQ-002 - Expressive, smooth axolotl movement and animation

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Expressive, smooth axolotl movement and animation
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Retain the character's distinct land and water movement, responsive transitions and readable actions. Polish is measured through motion continuity and play review rather than a claim of parity with Astro Bot.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-002-1** [automated] [PENDING] Land/water transitions retain the configured momentum, keep collision and visual pose aligned, and do not leave the player in the wrong movement grammar.
- **AC-REQ-002-2** [automated] [PENDING] Movement and facing accelerate and decelerate continuously at 30, 60 and 120 Hz; a swimming reversal turns through a bounded arc instead of snapping.
- **AC-REQ-002-3** [automated] [PENDING] Swimming folds the feet toward the body with a traveling body/tail wave; underwater idle preserves gentle foot motion; tail whack visibly reaches forward and has a bounded hit window.
- **AC-REQ-002-4** [manual] [PENDING] Review walking, hopping, climbing, swimming, turning, tail whack and spin charge in the production camera; underwater spin bubbles/current and land-slide surface disturbance communicate the action without obscuring threats.
- **AC-REQ-002-5** [automated] [PENDING] The character has exactly one authoritative physics-root writer; animation, backpack and surface effects cannot change the collision root, damage reach or movement velocity.
- **AC-REQ-002-6** [automated] [PENDING] At a fixed 60 Hz physics step, the same timed input replay rendered at 30, 60 and 120 FPS finishes within 5 percent of reference travel distance and 5 degrees of reference heading, with identical action and checkpoint events.
- **AC-REQ-002-7** [automated] [PENDING] Allowed locomotion inputs affect simulation within two physics ticks; buffered jump and interruption windows are configurable and tests cover their boundary ticks.
- **AC-REQ-002-8** [automated] [PENDING] A versioned animation transition table covers idle, start, travel, turn, stop, jump, fall, land, swim, surface, dive, attack, capture, dance and reset; every allowed edge exits without a stuck pose, duplicate action event or stale blend.
- **AC-REQ-002-9** [manual] [PENDING] Audit and approve the production rig, skin weights, clip coverage and contact chains; document whether each limb supports planted-foot solving and replace the current one-joint limb limitation before promising full foot locking.
- **AC-REQ-002-10** [manual] [PENDING] Approve a fixed production-camera reel at normal speed and slow motion covering starts, stops, reversals, slopes, landing, swimming transitions and moving attacks; resolve visible foot sliding, snapping, broken deformation and mechanical body/tail motion before declaring the character-polish milestone complete.
