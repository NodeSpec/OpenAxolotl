# Test Plan: REQ-004 - Nearby enemies with readable, smooth behavior

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Nearby enemies with readable, smooth behavior
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Netbots occupy land; Hooklines support land and underwater habitats. Encounters use perception, bounded patrols, telegraphs, committed attacks and readable recovery.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-004-1** [automated] [PENDING] An enemy acquires the player only inside its authored awareness range and valid line of sight, respects its home leash and habitat, and returns smoothly when pursuit ends.
- **AC-REQ-004-2** [automated] [PENDING] Attack wind-up, active contact and recovery are distinct; committed attacks do not rotate or home after release and cancelled attacks cannot retain active hit windows.
- **AC-REQ-004-3** [automated] [PENDING] A valid strike defeats an ordinary robot once per action, produces bounded mechanical debris, disables its attack/collision as appropriate, and cleans up debris.
- **AC-REQ-004-4** [manual] [PENDING] Production encounters are reviewed for smooth acceleration, turning and pose transitions, readable timing and space to evade.
- **AC-REQ-004-5** [automated] [PENDING] Enemy acceleration, heading and pose transitions use versioned habitat-specific tuning and pass the same timed pursuit/return replay at 30, 60 and 120 render FPS without different attack counts or out-of-habitat motion.
