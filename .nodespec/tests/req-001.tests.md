# Test Plan: REQ-001 - A complete single-player adventure before creation

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** A complete single-player adventure before creation
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Children can enjoy the shipped official adventure independently of the optional creator ecosystem. Parent-led creation extends a finished game rather than being required to make it playable.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-001-1** [automated] [PENDING] The shipped build starts and completes an official adventure without an AI account, creator tooling or a network connection.
- **AC-REQ-001-2** [automated] [PENDING] The game contains no multiplayer dependency; optional catalog failures do not prevent playing installed official content.
- **AC-REQ-001-3** [manual] [PENDING] A recorded family playtest evaluates the clarity and enjoyment of the official start-to-finish route; findings are resolved or explicitly tracked.
