# Test Plan: REQ-037 - Platformer jump feel and climb robustness

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Platformer jump feel and climb robustness
**Category:** functional
**Description:** The jump must feel fair and the climb must survive contact noise, because a platforming level is only as good as the forgiveness under it. None of these rules change where the axolotl CAN go on a perfectly-timed input; they change how much timing precision the player is asked for.

THREE FORGIVENESS RULES live in a JumpFeel class apart from the controller, pure arithmetic over time provable frame by frame with no scene: COYOTE TIME (a press just after the ledge still hops, the window expires, and firing spends it so it cannot chain into a double jump), JUMP BUFFERING (a press while falling is remembered on the rising edge only and fires on landing), and VARIABLE HEIGHT (releasing the button mid-rise cuts the climb, so one button expresses a range of arcs).

VARIABLE HEIGHT NEEDED A NEW INPUT FACT. Verbs are edge-triggered, so the hop verb is gone by the frame after launch, and a cut reading it would cut EVERY jump — measured in the greybox as a 2.02 m arc landing at 0.23 m. PlayerIntent therefore carries a second set, `sustained`, holding the verbs whose physical input is still down. It is re-resolved against the live grammar every frame, dropped on a device switch, and cleared on respawn, the same rules the held direction set follows.

THE CLIMB had two scene-tree bugs no unit test could see. Moving purely ALONG a wall generates no contact, so the climb detached on its second frame; the climber now presses into the wall at a tuned adhesion that move_and_slide absorbs. And losing the wall for a single frame — a seam, or the moment of cresting a lip — dropped the climber the whole way down; the controller now keeps the wall for a tuned contact grace after the body reports losing it.

THE ARC is retuned for platforming (impulse 9 m/s against 20 m/s² gravity, waddle 4.2 m/s) and measured in the live greybox: 1.81 m standing jump, 3.15 m running gap, the envelope every official level's geometry is derived from. Every window, the cut ratio, the adhesion and the grace are tuning keys with documented units and ranges (REQ-025); at zero each rule disables itself and the strict behaviour returns.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-037-1** [automated] [VERIFIED] Coyote time forgives a hop pressed just after leaving a ledge, expires after the tuned window, and is spent by firing so a hop can never chain into a mid-air ascent
- **AC-REQ-037-2** [automated] [VERIFIED] A hop pressed in mid-air is buffered on the rising edge only and fires on touchdown; the buffer expires rather than firing late, and holding the button does not refill it
- **AC-REQ-037-3** [automated] [VERIFIED] Releasing the hop mid-rise cuts the vertical speed by the tuned ratio; the cut never touches a descent, a rise the hop did not start, or a rise whose button is still held — proven through the real controller, where a tapped jump measurably rises less than a held one
- **AC-REQ-037-4** [automated] [VERIFIED] PlayerIntent carries a sustained verb set alongside the edge-triggered one: a held key sustains its verb until release, re-resolves under the live grammar (SPACE held through the water/land seam sustains what it means now), and is dropped on a device switch and on clear()
- **AC-REQ-037-5** [automated] [VERIFIED] A climber presses into the wall at the tuned adhesion on every frame — steering up, sideways, and clinging still — so motion along the surface cannot end the climb by starving it of contact, and the greybox smoke proves the climb rises in a real scene
- **AC-REQ-037-6** [automated] [VERIFIED] Losing wall contact for less than the tuned grace does not end a climb and regaining the wall resets the window; contact lost past the grace releases exactly once
- **AC-REQ-037-7** [automated] [VERIFIED] Every window, ratio and speed above is a tuning key with documented unit and range, and the greybox smoke and coral walk complete against the retuned arc
