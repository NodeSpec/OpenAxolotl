# Test Plan: REQ-017 - Desktop-first quality with a separate tablet decision

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Desktop-first quality with a separate tablet decision
**Category:** non-functional
**Description:** Ship a polished desktop experience first. Tablet play is a later feasibility decision; console certification and multiplayer are outside the current delivery scope.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-017-1** [manual] [PENDING] Name the desktop baseline hardware and approve numerical frame-time, memory, load-time and restoration-hitch budgets before release performance sign-off.
- **AC-REQ-017-2** [automated] [PENDING] A reproducible benchmark exercises the decorated first adventure with water, active enemies, debris and restoration and reports its measurements against the approved budgets.
- **AC-REQ-017-3** [manual] [PENDING] A tablet release is not committed until touch controls, UI readability, memory, sustained thermal/frame-time behavior and distribution constraints are demonstrated on named devices.
- **AC-REQ-017-4** [manual] [PENDING] Release notes distinguish tested platforms from experimental targets and do not equate a triangle ceiling with verified performance.
- **AC-REQ-017-5** [automated] [PENDING] The initial desktop quality target is 60 FPS with p95 frame time at most 16.7 ms, p99 at most 33.3 ms and no steady-play frame above 50 ms over a five-minute rendered benchmark after a declared warm-up; world entry is measured separately against 3000 ms. Hardware, resolution, renderer, quality preset, memory budget and capture overhead must be recorded before this can pass.
- **AC-REQ-017-6** [manual] [PENDING] Approve the character reel, representative decorated section and family-remix trial as separate quality gates; failure creates named corrective work and cannot be waived by merely passing functional tests.
