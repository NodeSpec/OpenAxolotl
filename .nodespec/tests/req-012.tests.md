# Test Plan: REQ-012 - Drift Fleet Enemy Roster

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Drift Fleet Enemy Roster
**Category:** functional
**Description:** The antagonist faction: faceless industrial extraction machinery, deliberately NOT human characters. This preserves every intended gameplay hook — nets, hooks, dredging, pollution — without making a real profession the villain, and it fits the restoration theme better, since the machinery that broke the habitat is exactly what the player disables to heal it. Each enemy is designed against a specific capability or system rather than being a generic damage-dealer. NETBOTS: drifting drone trawlers dragging ghost nets that entangle and strip swim-speed capability; countered with Jet Gills. HOOKLINE RIGS: patrolling barbed-lure rigs that snag and briefly strip the equipped Gill Mod; countered by spotting the line in murk with Glow Gills. DREDGERS: heavy bottom-scrapers that revert restored terrain to barren, with area-wipe attacks that cost a life. RUNOFF DRONES: toxin sprayers that cloud water, debuffing vision and gill recharge to push the player onto land routes and exercise the second movement grammar. Enemies are an optional Level Contract element and are registered extensibly so a world can supply its own.

THE PLAYER CAN FIGHT BACK, and the machines differ in how much fighting they take. Every unit declares a DURABILITY — how many strikes defeat it — so the roster reads as a range rather than as one health value: the Netbot falls to a single hit, the middle machines take two or three, and the Dredger, the only unit that can cost a life, is the toughest. A strike that does not finish a unit STAGGERS it: its effect is refused for a window and whatever it was already doing to the player is released, so the swing that lands on a Netbot is also how you get out of its net. The strike that reaches its durability DEFEATS it, and a defeated machine is inert for the rest of the attempt.

DEFEAT IS A KNOCK-OUT, NOT A DESTRUCTION, and that is a constraint rather than an unfinished edge. REQ-019 forbids depicting violence done to anything that reads as alive; these are machines, and one knocked over and left inert is the family-appropriate reading of the same beat. It also keeps a level a route problem: defeats reset when the player respawns at a checkpoint, so a level cannot be permanently emptied and then walked unopposed.

Durability is roster DATA like every other enemy property, so a world supplying its own machine picks its own without touching the enemy system core — the same extensibility claim AC-5 already rests on.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-012-1** [automated] [VERIFIED] Netbots entangle the player and strip swim-speed capability, and Jet Gills provide a functioning counter
- **AC-REQ-012-2** [automated] [VERIFIED] Hookline Rigs snag the player and strip the equipped Gill Mod for the window given by tuning key enemy.hookline.mod_strip_seconds, and Glow Gills reveal the line before it triggers
- **AC-REQ-012-3** [automated] [VERIFIED] Dredgers revert restored regions to barren, and their area-wipe attack decrements a life
- **AC-REQ-012-4** [automated] [VERIFIED] Runoff Drones apply a vision and gill-recharge debuff while the player is inside the toxin volume, with magnitudes and duration given by tuning keys enemy.runoff.vision_debuff_factor, enemy.runoff.gill_recharge_multiplier, and enemy.runoff.duration_s
- **AC-REQ-012-5** [automated] [VERIFIED] Enemies are registered through a documented extension interface, and a fixture enemy can be added without modifying any file in the enemy system core
- **AC-REQ-012-6** [automated] [VERIFIED] A world declaring no enemies remains contract-valid and fully completable
- **AC-REQ-012-7** [automated] [VERIFIED] Every Drift Fleet unit declares its durability in the roster, exactly one shipped unit falls to a single strike, and the shipped roster spans a range rather than sharing one value
- **AC-REQ-012-8** [automated] [VERIFIED] A strike below a unit's durability staggers it rather than defeating it: its effect is refused for the stagger window, whatever it was already doing to the player is released, and it recovers with the strikes it has taken remembered
- **AC-REQ-012-9** [automated] [VERIFIED] The strike that reaches a unit's durability defeats it, and a defeated unit refuses every effect lane for the rest of the attempt rather than recovering on a timer
- **AC-REQ-012-10** [automated] [VERIFIED] Defeats reset when the player respawns at a checkpoint, so a level cannot be permanently emptied
- **AC-REQ-012-11** [manual] [PENDING] Runoff Drone encounters make land routes read as the favorable path in hands-on play
- **AC-REQ-012-12** [manual] [PENDING] Whacking a machine reads as satisfying and readable to the maintainer in hands-on play: the stagger is visible, the defeat is unmistakable, and the durability differences are felt without being counted
