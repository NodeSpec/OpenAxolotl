# Test Plan: REQ-009 - A reusable gameplay kit with coherent presentation

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** A reusable gameplay kit with coherent presentation
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Give creators polished reusable gameplay components with reviewed art, collision, animation and documented parameters. The owner-curated kit includes separately approved assets and characters; acceptance into the kit does not publish a level.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-009-1** [automated] [PENDING] Each catalog entry has a stable ID/version, supported parameters, required dependencies, habitat/collision rules and rights/provenance metadata. Inclusion in the official creator kit requires an owner-authorized reusable-asset or character approval tied to its content digest and pinned dependencies; an adventure approval cannot substitute for it.
- **AC-REQ-009-2** [automated] [PENDING] Gameplay prefabs ship with appropriate collision, interaction volumes and bounded behavior; decorative motion cannot move gameplay collision.
- **AC-REQ-009-3** [manual] [PENDING] Land surfaces, shores, grass, rock, underwater depth gradients, waterfall origins, hero and enemies meet the starting-lagoon visual reference at gameplay distance without stretched pixelated textures or distracting repetition.
- **AC-REQ-009-4** [manual] [PENDING] Art and sound review checks action readability, sensible material scale, adjustable low-level background music and a distinct dance cue; asset and device budgets are recorded separately.
- **AC-REQ-009-5** [automated] [PENDING] The kit records mesh units, pivot/forward conventions, visual bounds, gameplay hulls, material scale and animation sockets; importing a revised model fails visibly when its required rig/socket contract is incompatible.
- **AC-REQ-009-6** [manual] [PENDING] Approve one representative shore-to-underwater section with final lighting, material blends, silhouette variety and collision before extending the same treatment across all sections.
- **AC-REQ-009-7** [manual] [PENDING] Approve a matched gameplay-camera lighting review of sunlit shallows, vegetation/root shade, cooler deep pools and sheltered root chambers: underwater light patterns affect appropriate surfaces and Skippy, shadows/caustics respect enclosure, vegetation preserves sightlines and collision clearance, and restoration improves water/plant/light presentation without obscuring routes or attacks.
