# Test Plan: REQ-010 - A small, approachable family level-creation workflow

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** A small, approachable family level-creation workflow
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Parents and children aged 5–9 privately remix a small complete example, with the parent operating accounts and AI tools. Codex/NodeSpec is a guided path; documented formats and validators also support other capable AI tools and direct Godot editing. Creation and submission are optional and separate from ordinary play.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-010-1** [automated] [PENDING] The starter plays from spawn to finish and demonstrates land, water, one enemy, checkpoint, collectible and one restoration or supported ability gate through the same public interfaces as official levels.
- **AC-REQ-010-2** [automated] [PENDING] A documented first remix changes one bounded part of the starter and passes conformance without editing core systems.
- **AC-REQ-010-3** [manual] [PENDING] A newcomer test covers setup, describing an idea, editing, previewing, checking and exporting; the 15–30 minute first-remix goal is measured after setup and reported honestly.
- **AC-REQ-010-4** [manual] [PENDING] Parent-facing instructions distinguish private local experimentation, reusable asset/character submissions, playable-level submissions and executable core contributions. They explain the parent's operator role, that submission does not guarantee publication, and that no child account or public profile is required; an observed family walkthrough confirms this is understandable.
- **AC-REQ-010-5** [manual] [PENDING] A family trial starts from a clean pinned-toolchain setup and records time, errors and assistance for a bounded AI-assisted remix and an equivalent direct-Godot edit; export and playtest must succeed without core changes.
